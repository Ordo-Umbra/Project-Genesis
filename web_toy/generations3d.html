γ is the load-bearing knob. The free energy is Σ(¼η⁴ − ½η²) + γ·Σa<bηa²ηb². Only γ > 1 makes the single-sector corners the minima, which is what creates walls. At γ = 0.5 the potential collapses to ¼|η|⁴ − ½|η|² — fully O(n) symmetric, ground state the whole sphere, no distinct phases at all. Slide it there and watch the structure die.

3-D has no steady state. Measured at N = 24–32: any bath below ≈0.35 coarsens to a single domain, anything above ≈0.5 is lattice-scale mush, and there is no window between. So this is a coarsening run with a finite informative lifetime — hence the reseed. Run-to-run spread is large and is set by the initial draw, not by the bath: at a fixed noise, four seeds gave domain scales from 1.2 to full monopoly, and the pattern was the same at noise 0.005 and 0.05. Watch a few runs, not one.
