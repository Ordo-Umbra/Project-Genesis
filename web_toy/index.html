The core idea

A scalar field φ evolves by gradient-ascent on S = ΔC + κΔI — the balance of distinction (making differences) and integration (binding them coherently). The update rule is a 2-D version of the URP field equation:
∂ₜφ = D·∇²φ  +  β·|∇φ|²  −  w·sin(2π·k·φ)
The β·|∇φ|² term amplifies gradients (distinction); the diffusion D·∇²φ smooths (integration); the multi-well term w·sin(2π·k·φ) is the wall tension that lets the medium settle into k distinct domains — β-sectorisation. At k = 3 the three domains are coloured red / green / blue — the seed of the three-sector colour SU(3) picture, with the cell borders standing in for gluon-like domain walls. (A genuine 3-way tiling with the theory's 120° Y-junctions needs the full 3-component sector field Ψ∈ℂ³; a single scalar field gives layered domains, which is itself an honest result about what this reduced model can and can't show.)
